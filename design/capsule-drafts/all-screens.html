Plain and Simple Monthly Capsule

Once a month

Friends write once a month.
You get one capsule.

A private page for a small group. Everyone sends a short letter and a few photos during the month. When the window closes, it all arrives as one thing to read.

Landing does two jobs only. Sign in, or start a group. There is no public sign-up and no group directory — people join by opening a share link, so nothing on this page asks for a group name or PIN.

How a month goes

  3. The window closes and one capsule goes out.

Manage your groups

The email is the capsule, not a teaser for it. Full letters go in the body so it reads in the inbox with no click. The link at the end is for people who would rather read it on the site or send it on.
Build constraints: tables and inline styles, no web fonts, images with real alt text, and a text-only alternative part. Long months will clip in Gmail — put the contents list and “read the whole capsule” link near the top for those.
Everyone is bcc'd so the group's addresses never travel in a header. Replies land with the owner, and the reply-to copy says so.
